About

Built for MCA students

Master of Computer Applications (MCA) is a two-year professional master's degree in computer science awarded in India. The program is designed to meet the growing demand for qualified professionals in the field of Information Technology.

This hub brings together class notes, university question papers, syllabus copies and lab programs across all four semesters of the KTU MCA curriculum — organized, searchable and free to access.

  • University: APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU)
  • Program: Master of Computer Applications
  • Duration: 2 years · 4 semesters
  • Community: CET MCA Community
